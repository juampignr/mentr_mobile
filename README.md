# Mentr

**A Wikipedia explorer for people who learn by wandering.**

</div>

---

## Learning should be guided, not instructed.

Traditional education floods you with information regardless of what you're actually curious about. Inspired by John Dewey's philosophy of *facilitated* (not instructed) learning, Mentr lets you follow your own intuition — moving from the general to the particular, one topic at a time.

No courses. No curricula. Just you, Wikipedia, and wherever your curiosity takes you.

## Features

- **Interest tracking** — Mentr quietly notices what you spend time on, no input required
- **Ranked home screen** — your top interest chains, surfaced automatically
- **Hidden-link discovery** — a long-press mechanic that goes beyond a single article's surface links to recommend genuinely related topics
- **Fully private, account-free** — everything lives on your device, nothing is sent anywhere
- **Backup & restore** — export your data manually and bring it to another device whenever you want

## Privacy first

Mentr has no accounts and no server. All data — your interests, your reading history, everything — is stored locally in SQLite on your device. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. You control your data via manual export/import, and that's it.

## Tech stack

- [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/) + [Expo](https://expo.dev/) (EAS Build)
- [`expo-sqlite`](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/sqlite/) for local, on-device persistence
- [Wikipedia Action API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Main_page) for content and topic graphs
- [`expo-sharing`](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/sharing/) for manual backup/export
